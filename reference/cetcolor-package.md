# cetcolor: CET Perceptually Uniform Colour Maps

Collection of perceptually uniform colour maps made by Peter Kovesi
(2015) "Good Colour Maps: How to Design Them"
[arXiv:1509.03700](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03700) at the Centre for
Exploration Targeting (CET).

## Details

56 Colour maps are available as of May 2018
