## Additional details

    CET Color Maps were developed by Peter Kovesi and released under CC-BY 4.0

    The cetcolor package provides an integration of the CET color maps into R.
    cetcolor accomplishes this by supplying graphic functions, examples, and more.
    The R implementation was done by James Joseph Balamuta with additional
    contributions by contributors listed in the ChangeLog.
